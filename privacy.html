Buck Ledger privacy

Short version: your photos and your spots never leave your phone.

  • No accounts and no sign-up.
  • No ads, no analytics, no trackers, no third-party scripts.
  • Photos you log, camera names, buck names, tags and your property location are stored only on your device, in your browser's storage. They are never uploaded to us or anyone else.
  • Your property location is only used on the phone to work out sunrise, sunset and shooting light. "Use my location" asks your phone once and saves the result on the phone.
  • After the first load the app works offline and sends none of your data anywhere.
  • One exception: when you enter a Pro license key, the app sends that key (with the product ID, and nothing else) to Gumroad's license service to confirm it's a valid, non-refunded purchase. While online it re-checks the key about once a month. Gumroad handles this under its own privacy policy.
  • Purchases, support and refunds are handled through Gumroad.
  • Backups and CSV exports are files you create and keep yourself. A license isn't copied into backup files.
  • Clearing this site's data in your browser deletes everything the app stored, so keep a backup.
